WHO WE ARE

About CIY-26

Creative and Innovative Youth 2026 (CIY-26) is a youth-driven group focused on creativity, technology, innovation and practical solutions to challenges facing our society.

We bring together young people with different skills and interests in areas such as Artificial Intelligence, Web Development, Arduino and IoT, Data Science, Signal Processing and Industrial Automation.

Meet Our Team

Our Vision

To build a generation of creative and innovative young people capable of creating practical solutions for their communities and the future.

Our Mission

To empower young people through technology, innovation, creativity, skills development and collaborative projects.

Core Values

Creativity, teamwork, integrity, innovation, learning, responsibility and community impact.
